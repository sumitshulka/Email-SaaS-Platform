# Global Company Intelligence — Implementation Specification

## 1. Objective

Extend the existing Global Company DB with an on-demand **Company Intelligence** capability.

The system must NOT research companies automatically.

The Intelligence/Research workflow must execute **only when the user explicitly clicks the `Research` button for a specific company**.

The purpose of Company Intelligence is to create a reusable, structured intelligence profile that can later be used by:

- AI email generation
- Lead qualification
- Prospect scoring
- Campaign personalization
- Sales recommendations
- Company discovery
- Future AI sales workflows

The research should be stored against the company and reused until it becomes stale.

---

# 2. Core Principle

There are two separate layers:

### Layer 1 — Global Company DB

Contains company master information.

Examples:

- Company name
- Website
- Industry
- Country
- Address
- Employee range
- Company identifiers
- Other manually entered company data

This data should NOT require AI research.

### Layer 2 — Company Intelligence

AI-generated research about the company.

This is created/updated ONLY through the Research button.

```text
Global Company DB
        │
        │ User clicks Research
        ▼
Research / Intelligence Engine
        │
        ├── Web Research
        ├── LLM Analysis
        ├── Fact Extraction
        ├── Signal Detection
        └── Opportunity Analysis
        │
        ▼
Company Intelligence JSON
        │
        ▼
Stored against Company
```

---

# 3. Research Trigger

The Research function must NEVER execute automatically when:

- A company is created
- A company is edited
- A user searches for a company
- A company is selected
- An email is created
- A user opens the company profile
- A user logs in
- A campaign is created

The only standard trigger is:

### `Research` button

Example:

```text
Company List

Oracle
Technology
USA

[View] [Research]
```

When the user clicks:

**Research**

show a confirmation/modal:

> Research this company using AI?
>
> This will use AI research resources and update the company's intelligence profile.
>
> [Cancel] [Start Research]

After confirmation, execute the intelligence workflow.

---

# 4. Research Button States

The button should have different states.

### No research exists

```text
[ Research ]
```

### Research in progress

```text
[ Researching... ]
```

Button must be disabled.

### Research completed

```text
[ View Intelligence ] [ Re-research ]
```

### Research is stale

```text
[ Research Update ]
```

### Research failed

```text
[ Retry Research ]
```

# 5. Research Frequency / Cost Protection

Do NOT allow repeated accidental research.

Store:

```json
{
  "research_status": "completed",
  "researched_at": "2026-10-07T18:00:00Z",
  "research_version": "1.0"
}
```

If the user clicks Research again within the configured freshness period, show:

> This company was researched recently on 7 Oct 2026.
>
> Re-researching will consume AI research resources.
>
> [Cancel] [Research Again]

Do not block deliberate re-research.

The user can always explicitly override.

Recommended default freshness:

**30 days**

This should be configurable by Superadmin.

---

# 6. Research Workflow

When Research is triggered:

### Step 1 — Load company master record

Retrieve:

- Company ID
- Company name
- Website
- Industry
- Country
- Existing company description
- Any other existing Global DB information

### Step 2 — Research external sources

Research should prioritize:

1. Official company website
2. Official company product/service pages
3. Official company newsroom/press releases
4. Investor relations pages, where applicable
5. Regulatory/public filings where applicable
6. Reliable business/industry sources
7. Other credible sources

Do NOT rely exclusively on search snippets.

### Step 3 — Collect research evidence

The research engine should collect relevant evidence before asking the LLM to produce the final intelligence profile.

### Step 4 — LLM analysis

The LLM should transform the evidence into structured intelligence.

### Step 5 — Validate JSON

The LLM response must conform to the predefined JSON schema.

Do not store arbitrary LLM output.

### Step 6 — Save intelligence

Save the structured intelligence against the Company ID.

### Step 7 — Save source references

Every externally derived claim should be traceable to one or more sources.

### Step 8 — Update research metadata

Record:

- researched_at
- research version
- model used
- research status
- source count
- research duration
- token usage if available
- estimated research cost if available

# 7. Intelligence Data Model

Do NOT store intelligence as a single large text field.

Use structured JSON.

Recommended top-level structure:

```json
{
  "company_id": "CMP-000123",

  "research": {
    "version": "1.0",
    "status": "completed",
    "researched_at": "2026-10-07T18:00:00Z",
    "valid_until": "2026-11-06T18:00:00Z",

    "executive_summary": {},

    "company_profile": {},

    "business": {},

    "technology": {},

    "current_signals": [],

    "opportunity_signals": [],

    "sales_intelligence": {},

    "competitors": [],

    "sources": []
  }
}
```

# 12. Current Signals

This is a critical section.

Every signal MUST be dated.

Never store an undated current signal.

```json

  "type": "expansion",
  "title": "",
  "description": "",
  "event_date": "2026-09-20",
  "source_published_date": "2026-09-20",
  "observed_at": "2026-10-07T18:00:00Z",
  "importance": "high",
  "sales_relevance": [],
  "confidence": 0.92,
  "source_ids": ["SRC-001"]
}
```

Possible signal types:

```text
expansion
acquisition
funding
partnership
product_launch
technology_adoption
ai_initiative
digital_transformation

market_entry
contract
leadership_change
cost_reduction
```

# 13. Opportunity Signals

Opportunity signals are AI interpretations, NOT facts.

Schema:

```json
{
  "opportunity_id": "OPP-001",
  "type": "potential_pain_point",
  "statement": "",
  "basis": [],
  "confidence": 0.71,
  "generated_at": "2026-10-07T18:00:00Z",
  "source_ids": []
}
```

Possible types:

```text
potential_pain_point
growth_opportunity
technology_gap
operational_complexity
digital_transformation
cost_optimization
compliance
inventory_complexity
workforce
data_modernization
ai_adoption
```

Important rule:

AI inference must never be presented as a confirmed company fact.

Example:

BAD:

> Oracle has inventory visibility problems.

GOOD:

> Oracle's large distributed operations may create opportunities for enhanced inventory visibility.

# 17. Source Reliability

Use:

```text
high
medium
low
```

Recommended logic:

### High

Official company website, regulatory filing, government source.

### Medium

Established business publication or credible industry publication.

### Low

Aggregators, directories, blogs or unverified sources.

Low-confidence sources should not be used for strong personalization claims.

---

# 18. No Key People

Do NOT create or store a `key_people` section in Company Intelligence.

People change too frequently and should eventually be handled through a separate contact/prospect intelligence module.

Company Intelligence should remain focused on the company.

---

# 19. Research Freshness

Store:

```json
{
  "researched_at": "2026-10-07T18:00:00Z",
  "valid_until": "2026-11-06T18:00:00Z",
  "freshness_status": "fresh"
}
```

Possible statuses:

```text
fresh
aging
stale
```

Recommended:

```text
0–30 days = fresh
31–90 days = aging
91+ days = stale
```

These thresholds should be configurable.

# 24. Error Handling

If research fails:

Do not delete the existing intelligence.

Example:

```text
Existing research:
07 Sep 2026

New research:
Failed

Result:
Keep 07 Sep research as current intelligence.
Mark latest research attempt as failed.
```

Show:

> Research could not be completed. Existing intelligence has been retained.

This prevents a failed API call from destroying useful company intelligence.

---

# 25. Duplicate Research Protection

Prevent simultaneous research jobs for the same company.

If:

```text
Company = Oracle
Research status = running
```

another Research request should return:

> Research is already in progress for this company.

Do not launch a second job.

---

# 26. LLM Prompt Requirements

The research-analysis LLM must be explicitly instructed:

1. Use only supplied research evidence.
2. Do not invent facts.
3. Distinguish facts from inference.
4. Every current signal must have a date.
5. Every externally derived claim must have a source.
6. Do not invent people.
7. Do not infer technology usage without evidence.
8. Do not create unsupported revenue/employee figures.
9. Use confidence scores.
10. Return ONLY valid JSON matching the defined schema.

The backend must validate the JSON before storing it.

---

# 27. Research Quality Rules

The LLM should prioritize:

**Official source > regulatory/government source > credible business source > industry source > other source**

If conflicting information exists:

- retain the higher-quality source
- flag the conflict
- reduce confidence
- do not arbitrarily choose a number

---

# 28. Important Cost-Control Rule

Research must be **company-level**, not email-level.

Once Company A has intelligence:

```text
User 1 → Oracle → use existing intelligence
User 2 → Oracle → use existing intelligence
User 3 → Oracle → use existing intelligence
```

No new research should occur unless:

- intelligence is stale AND
- the user explicitly requests research

or:

- user explicitly chooses `Re-research`.

The email-generation system must NEVER automatically trigger fresh company research in V1.

Keep the following components separate:

```text
Company Master
      │
      ├── Company data
      │
      ▼
Company Intelligence
      │
      ├── Research profile
      ├── Signals
      ├── Opportunities
      └── Sources

      │
      └── Execution/cost/history
```

# 32. V1 Acceptance Criteria

The implementation is complete only when all of the following work:

### AC-01

Creating a company does NOT trigger AI research.

### AC-02

Opening a company does NOT trigger AI research.

### AC-03

Searching the Global Company DB does NOT trigger AI research.

### AC-04

Only clicking `Research` starts a research job.

### AC-05

Research status is visible.

### AC-06

Only one research job can run for a company at a time.

### AC-07

Research produces schema-valid structured JSON.

### AC-08

Every current signal contains a date.

### AC-09

Every external claim has a source reference.

### AC-10

AI inferences are clearly separated from facts.

### AC-11

Key people are NOT stored.

### AC-12

Failed research does not destroy previous successful research.

### AC-13

Research history is retained.

### AC-14

Research usage/cost is recorded.

### AC-15

Fresh intelligence is reused rather than automatically re-researched.

### AC-16

Email generation can later consume the existing intelligence without triggering new research.

### AC-17

Superadmin can see research usage and costs.

### AC-18

The system never silently performs research in the background.

# 34. Most Important Product Rule

**Do not make the Research button merely generate a summary.**

It should create a reusable **Company Intelligence Asset**.

The summary is just the UI representation.

The underlying structured intelligence is what will later power the entire AI sales/email platform.

End state:

```text
GLOBAL COMPANY DB
       │
       │ Manual / imported company data
       ▼
COMPANY RECORD
       │
       │ Explicit Research click
       ▼
COMPANY INTELLIGENCE
       │
       ├── Facts
       ├── Business
       ├── Technology
       ├── Dated Signals
       ├── AI Opportunities
       ├── Sales Intelligence
       ├── Competitors
       └── Sources
       │
       ▼
REUSABLE AI KNOWLEDGE
       │
       ├── Email Generation
       ├── Lead Scoring
       ├── Campaign Personalization
       ├── Prospect Discovery
       └── Future AI Sales Agent
```

The **Global Company DB is the asset**, and **Research is an explicit, metered enrichment action**. No company should incur an AI research cost simply because it exists in the database.
