name: Mailflow brand direction
description: Approved audience, personality, color reference, logo direction, and typography for Mailflow.
---

Mailflow is primarily for small-business marketing teams. Its approved brand personality is confident and dependable.

Use the Flowline identity: a compact message-route mark that subtly forms an “M” and ends in a forward arrow, paired with a lowercase Mailflow wordmark in Plus Jakarta Sans.

Use Taskone’s text/logo colors as inspiration, especially deep navy and confident blue with red as an energetic counterpoint. Do not copy Taskone’s logo.

**Why:** The user explicitly selected this audience, personality, color reference, and identity direction.

**How to apply:** Keep future Mailflow brand assets, prominent product branding, and typography consistent with this direction unless the user requests a rebrand.
