---
name: Basic-plan support requests
description: User-defined eligibility and timing rules for one-month Basic subscription support grants.
---

Allow up to three Basic-plan support requests per user, in milestone order:
1. The first request is available during the user's first uninterrupted Free-plan period.
2. The second request requires at least three completed calendar months of continuous paid primary-plan access.
3. The third request requires at least six completed calendar months of continuous paid primary-plan access.

The user must be on an active Free plan to submit. Paid tenure is continuous, not cumulative across gaps; paid support grants and other no-payment gifts do not count as paid time. Pending requests block another application. Admins may decide within 72 hours; any request still pending at the deadline is automatically granted, even if an admin was online. Manual rejection requires a reason and consumes that milestone. An approved grant lasts 30 days, then the user's Free plan resumes.

**Why:** the user specified lifetime limits, non-consecutive requests, continuous paid-month thresholds, a 72-hour decision window, and an automatic grant for unresolved requests.

**How to apply:** preserve this policy in eligibility checks, subscription scheduling, admin decisions, notifications, and user-facing Plans UI.
