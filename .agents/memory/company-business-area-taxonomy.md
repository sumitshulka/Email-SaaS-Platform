---
name: Company business-area taxonomy
description: Mailflow's intended distinction between business area, specific industry, and technical website/email matching domain.
---

In Mailflow company profiles, “Domain” means a broad, non-unique business area such as Manufacturing, Education, Textiles, or Service Industry. Platform superadmins control the selectable values. Industry remains the more specific field, such as Steel Fabrication. The website/email domain remains a separate technical identifier for matching.

**Why:** the user explicitly defined this taxonomy and chose platform superadmins to manage the allowed business-area list.

**How to apply:** keep business area as a non-unique selectable classification; do not use it for company deduplication or contact auto-linking. Preserve the technical domain and website-based matching behavior.
